# Literature collection

Review date: 14 October 2026.

## Scope and collection

The collection follows six topics: sequence architectures, internal computation, planning and metareasoning, continual learning, world models, and proactive agents. It includes foundational methods and recent architectural neighbors.

Search began with the project capabilities and known papers, then expanded through primary publication records, references, and related mechanisms. Queries covered recurrent latent reasoning, persistent embodied state, planning budgets, test-time memory, continual computation, incremental replanning, dual control, and goal exploration.

For each source, the catalogue records its mechanism, evaluated tasks, relevance to a model choice, inspected sections, and reading priority. Records are deduplicated by publication identity, usually an arXiv identifier or canonical URL. Years follow the cited edition or a recorded initial release.

## Reading depth

| Value | Material inspected |
| --- | --- |
| `abstract` | Primary abstract and bibliographic record |
| `full_text` | Full-paper sections identified in `inspected_sections` |
| `official_summary` | Original account maintained by the authors or institution |
| `indexed_excerpt` | Indexed primary-source passage when direct retrieval was unavailable |

The collection has 108 records: 35 section-level full-text readings, 68 abstracts, four official summaries, and one indexed excerpt. This is a curated collection. It records reading rather than independent reproduction.

Schema version 2 records reading depth in `reading_depth` and source sections in `inspected_sections`.

## Continuing the survey

Read method, training, and experiment sections before implementing a close baseline. Pin the paper edition and code revision. Expand abstract-level records as that reading is completed.

Add a paper when it changes an architecture choice, comparison, training method, or experiment. Update the corresponding review and model plan. Repeat recent-work searches before manuscript submission and record which comparisons were actually run.

Statements in the reviews describe the inspected source material. Missing discussion of a capability means that it was outside the recorded reading, rather than establishing that the method cannot support it.
