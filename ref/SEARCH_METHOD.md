# Search Scope and Evidence Method

**Review date and cutoff: 14 October 2026.**

## Scope

This collection covers six intersecting lines: sequence architectures and memory; internal computation and real-time execution; planning and metareasoning; continual and meta-learning; world models and embodied interaction; and proactive exploration and agent systems. The scope follows CDM's capabilities rather than a single architecture name.

The search includes foundational papers and recent work available by the cutoff. Historical sources matter when a proposed neural mechanism resembles an established algorithmic idea. Recent preprints matter when they are direct architectural or experimental neighbors.

## Retrieval procedure

1. Start from the project requirements and known reference papers, including attention–recurrence hybrids, latent reasoning, world models, and continual learning.
2. Search primary publication records and exact titles; expand to cited predecessors and nearby architectural or task formulations.
3. Perform recent-work searches for terms such as `recurrent latent reasoning 2026`, `persistent state vision language action`, `real-time learning planning budgets`, `test-time memory`, and `continuous memory machines`.
4. Search older conceptual neighbors using terms such as `continual computation`, `metareasoning value of computation`, `incremental replanning`, `dual control`, and `intrinsically motivated goal exploration`.
5. Open original papers, author publications, conference proceedings, or official research pages. Inspect method and experiment sections for the closest comparisons.
6. Record the source, inspected location, mechanism, demonstrated scope, and consequence for a CDM decision.

Exact-name searches were also used to check terminology. A project's name or acronym is not treated as evidence of conceptual novelty or exclusivity.

## Inclusion and organization

A paper is included when it establishes a relevant mechanism, offers a close alternative explanation for a proposed contribution, provides an informative evaluation setting, or clarifies a necessary distinction. The bibliography therefore includes both direct model competitors and intellectual foundations.

Search snippets, secondary summaries, and social posts generally serve as discovery aids. Where direct access failed and a publisher-indexed abstract or indexed primary excerpt supplied limited evidence, the record explicitly identifies that retrieval route. These entries are not treated as method-level comparisons. Benchmark leadership is not repeated as a timeless property. Results are interpreted within the model, task, data, and resource conditions actually reported.

Sources are deduplicated by their underlying publication, principally the arXiv identifier or canonical publication URL. A work may appear in several thematic discussions while retaining one catalogue record. The recorded year refers to the cited edition or explicitly documented initial release; later revisions are noted where relevant.

## Evidence labels

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `abstract` | The primary abstract and bibliographic record were inspected. Claims are limited to what that record supports. |
| `full_text` | The full-paper sections named in `evidence_location` were inspected. These may be methods or experiments, or only introductory sections for conceptual sources. This is section-level evidence, not complete-paper reading or independent reproduction. |
| `official_summary` | An author or institution's original account was inspected when that is the cited evidence. |
| `indexed_excerpt` | An indexed excerpt of a primary source was available while direct access failed; only the recorded limited statement is supported. |

`reading_priority` distinguishes direct comparisons from supporting foundations. It is a project-specific reading order, not a quality ranking.

An unreported capability is recorded as outside the inspected evidence. It is not interpreted as proof that the method cannot support that capability. The comparison document consequently uses explicit descriptions rather than filling unsupported cells with crosses.

## Boundaries of this review

This is a curated, source-checked research map, not an exhaustive bibliometric review. Coverage is broad across the relevant mechanisms; reading depth is intentionally greater for direct neighbors. Some records support discovery and reading order through abstracts, while the key novelty comparisons require method-level reading.

No paper result in this collection has been independently reproduced as part of the literature-writing task. Code availability, source access, and reported performance are separate facts. Publication metadata in the BibTeX file is deliberately limited to verified fields; arXiv records are not assigned invented conference acceptances.

## Updating the collection

Add a source when it changes a mechanism choice, baseline, interpretation, or evaluation. Update the corresponding comparison and experiment rather than merely extending a list of titles. Before preparing a submission, repeat the recent-work search, pin the versions of the closest papers and implementations, and replace proposed comparisons with measured results.

Maintain the distinction between the full CDM objective and the claim of a particular paper. New overlap can narrow a claim or improve a baseline without changing the broader research goal.
